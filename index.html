A protocol is always described as one that records no token in the snapshot — the snapshot cannot prove it has none. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of a token that already trades are excluded from the board: they can never launch a token of their own. Implied values are this radar's own claim, never a promise of a launch. The odds are a chance of a token within 24 months given no token yet — old protocols that never launched score low on purpose, because the survivors are the ones least likely to launch next.
